Florin Cards Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

Florin Cards is an idle card-collecting game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: the game has no accounts, your progress stays on your phone, and the only third parties involved are Google (for ads and purchases) and GitHub (for a game settings file).

Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal information, and the game does not ask you to create an account.

  • Game progress (florins, cards, settings and so on) is saved only on your device. It is never uploaded to us.
  • Save codes: if you tap Copy my code in Settings, the game puts a copy of your save on your clipboard. What you do with it is up to you; we never receive it.
  • Gameplay statistics: the game records anonymous gameplay events (for example "pack opened") in a small log that stays on your device and is not sent anywhere. You can turn this off under Settings → Share anonymous stats. If a future version sends anonymous statistics to an analytics service, we will update this policy first.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game only shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example to double your income). It has no banner or pop-up ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and how you interact with the ad, to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud.

  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before any ads load, where you can choose how your data is used.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Google Play)

Optional in-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game receives only a purchase record from Google Play (which product was bought and a purchase token) so it can give you the item. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Nothing sold for real money or gems has random contents. Card packs come only from playing, and their exact odds are shown in the game.

Game settings download (GitHub)

When it starts, the game downloads a small settings file (event dates and balance values) from GitHub. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to GitHub. No other information is sent, and we do not receive request logs. GitHub's privacy statement: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders (for example "A free pack is waiting!") are scheduled on your phone. No notification server is involved. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Children

Florin Cards is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Because your progress is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset game, or by uninstalling the game. Data collected by Google for ads or purchases is kept according to Google's policies, and you can manage it from your Google account and phone settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: open an issue at github.com/Funfetsunk/florin-cards/issues.
